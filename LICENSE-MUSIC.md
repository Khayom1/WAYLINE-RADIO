# Music and media rights policy

WAYLINE RADIO separates software licensing from music and media rights.

For every track, retain:
- track title, performer/artist, composer and rights holder;
- the exact license or written permission;
- source and proof URL or document reference;
- permission for **commercial use**;
- permission for **internet streaming / public communication**;
- permitted territories, audience and term, including an expiry date;
- attribution text required by the license.

A Creative Commons label alone is not enough: check the specific variant and all conditions. In particular, do not assume CC BY-NC permits commercial radio, and do not treat a download link as a broadcast license. Public-domain status must be checked for both the composition and the particular sound recording in the relevant territory.

The application blocks a track by default. An administrator must inspect and record the actual proof before marking the rights verified. This metadata gate is an operational safeguard, not legal advice or an independent rights verification service. If the license scope is ambiguous, do not broadcast the track.
